# App name

Sesli Rehber — Accessible Assistant

# Short description

Turkish voice phone assistant with an on-device motion-awareness pilot.

# Full description

Sesli Rehber is a controlled pilot designed to help blind and low-vision users start common phone actions by voice and notice motion through the camera.

It can provide time, date and battery information; control the torch and media volume; prepare calls, messages, walking directions, location sharing and alarms; and ask for missing contact, destination, message or time details. Critical actions require spoken confirmation.

The Hareket Görüş screen processes moving image regions on device and displays tracks plus approximate image-plane speed and acceleration. Camera frames are not uploaded to a developer server.

Important: This alpha does not reliably classify vehicles, walls, curbs, potholes or safe crossing directions. It does not replace a white cane, guide dog or professional orientation and mobility training, and must not be used as an independent traffic-safety system.
