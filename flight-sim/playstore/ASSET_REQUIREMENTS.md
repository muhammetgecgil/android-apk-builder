# Google Play Store Asset Requirements

Reference date: 2026-08-30. Always re-check Play Console before final production upload.

## Required listing graphics
- App icon: 512 x 512 px, 32-bit PNG with alpha, max 1024 KB.
- Feature graphic: 1024 x 500 px, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha.
- Screenshots: at least 2 screenshots are required across supported device types. JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha; each dimension 320–3840 px and the long side must not exceed 2x the short side.

## Recommended screenshot set for Aircraft Simulator 3D
1. Hangar start with aircraft centered and hangar exit visible.
2. Taxiway with centerline and blue edge lights.
3. RWY 27 takeoff roll.
4. Rotation/climb with coastline visible.
5. Scenic sea flight.
6. Mountain/valley flight.
7. Final approach aligned with RWY 27.
8. Landing/rollout.

## Visual rules
- Do not claim ranking, awards, price promotions or “#1 / Best”.
- Do not imply official affiliation with an aircraft manufacturer, armed force or government organization.
- Keep text minimal and important content near the center.
- Use a consistent visual language between icon, feature graphic and screenshots.
- Avoid real trademarks unless you own or are licensed to use them.

## Optional preview video
A YouTube preview video can be added. Recommended story: hangar → taxi → takeoff → coastline → scenic flight → approach → landing, using actual in-app footage.
