# v19.1 Aircraft Art Acceptance Gate

A replacement model is accepted only when all gates pass:

- Correct silhouette and believable real-world proportions.
- Real metre scale and centered aircraft origin.
- Separate canopy, stabilators, rudders, flaperons, nozzles and landing gear.
- Hinge pivots located correctly for animation.
- PBR materials; no flat single-color aircraft skin.
- Clean normals/tangents and no obvious shading seams.
- Three practical LODs for mobile rendering.
- Texture budget appropriate for high-end Android with lower-quality fallback.
- Verified redistribution/commercial licence before Play Store release.
- 360-degree visual inspection: nose, side, top, rear and close-up.
- Sustained frame-time test on device before replacing the fallback renderer.

Target: a convincing aircraft at normal chase-camera distance, not merely a high polygon count.
